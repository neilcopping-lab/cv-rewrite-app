Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The CV Builder comes free with your Comquat account, along with CV Rewrite and Interview Prep. No card, no trial that turns into a bill.

I've never written a CV. Can I still use it?

That's exactly who Create your 1st CV is for. Saturday jobs, volunteering, school and college, the things you do outside of all that: it all counts, and the questions help you find it.

Will it make things up to sound impressive?

No. It writes from what you tell it, in your words. You read everything before it goes on your CV, and you tick to say it's all true before you download.

Can I stop and come back?

Yes. It saves as you work. Open the CV Builder from My account and carry on where you left off.

How does it work with CV Rewrite?

Build one full CV here, then press Tailor it for a job. CV Rewrite picks up your CV, you add the job advert, and it's rewritten for that role.
