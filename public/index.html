Questions

Is this actually AI, or a template with my name stuck in?

It's a real rewrite built from your specific CV and the actual advert each time, not a fixed template. We use AI to tidy grammar and sharpen your sentences, never to write your personal statement or career story in its own voice. If we can't ground something in what you told us, we say so plainly rather than filling it with filler.

Will it make things up to sound impressive?

No, and we built a hard check specifically to stop that. Every number, employer, title, qualification and skill in the finished CV has to trace back to something you actually provided. Anything that doesn't gets stripped out and shown to you as a question to answer, not quietly left in.

Is my data safe?

Uploaded files are deleted from our server as soon as the text has been pulled out. Same policy as the Interview Prep Report.

What's the ATS version?

Online application portals parse your CV with software before a human sees it. The ATS version strips your chosen design down to a single column with standard headings, no tables, no icons and no header or footer, so it parses cleanly. You get it from the same design you picked.

What if I need to change something after I've paid?

You can regenerate and re-download as many times as you need in the same session. Get in touch if you need it re-run after that.

How is the rest of the site still free?

Every guide, dispatch and Loudspeaker email stays free, always. This is the one thing we charge for: doing the rewrite for you rather than teaching you to do it yourself.
