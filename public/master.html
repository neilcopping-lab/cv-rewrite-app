2. A few lines about you

Three or four sentences: what you do, what you're good at, and what you want next. Write it the way you'd say it to someone at work. CV Rewrite will sharpen it for each job.

3. Work history

Start with your most recent job and work back. Put everything in, even jobs you'd leave off some applications: CV Rewrite picks what fits each job. One line per task or result. Numbers help, like team sizes, targets and what changed because of you.

4. Key skills

The things you're good at, each with a line of proof if you can. "Rota planning: ran rotas for a team of 20 for three years" beats "excellent organiser".

5. Education and training

Qualifications, apprenticeships, courses and certificates. Food hygiene, first aid and licences count.

6. Interests (optional)

One per line. Specific beats general: "Coaching under-11s football" says more than "sport".
