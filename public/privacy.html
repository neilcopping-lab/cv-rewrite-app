Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Only you can see your account, your CVs and your answers. Nobody can message you through Comquat.
  • Emails stay off unless you tick them. We never sell your details, show you adverts or use your data to train AI.
  • Before anything goes to an AI, we take out your name, email, phone number, address and links.
  • Practice videos stay on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.
  • You can download everything, or delete it all, from your account page whenever you like. If you don't use your account for 12 months, we close it, after two emails with a button to keep it.
  • Comquat is for people aged 16 and over. If you're under 18, tell a parent, carer or teacher you're using it. Worried about anything? Get help or report a concern.

The full details are below.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Comquat CV Rewrite at cv.the-common-people.com. We keep it plain, and we keep your data for as little time as we can.

Who we are

Comquat CV Rewrite is run by Comquat Ventures UK Ltd, a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 17581616). Comquat is the CV Rewrite and Interview Prep app from The Com'mon People. We are the "data controller" for the information described here.

Contact for any privacy question or request: neil.copping@thecommonpeople.co.uk.
Post: Comquat Ventures UK Ltd, 102 Stevenage Road, Hitchin, SG4 9DQ (our registered office).

What we collect

  • The CV and job advert you upload or paste.
  • Any answers you give to our questions, typed or recorded by voice, and any answers you give for a cover letter.
  • A photo, only if you choose to add one to your CV.
  • Your name and email address, held in your Comquat account so you can sign in (see Your Comquat account below), and used, if you ask, to email your finished CV to you.
  • A record that you ticked the box to confirm you'd checked your CV or cover letter before downloading it.
  • Any feedback you choose to leave: a star rating and an optional comment.
  • One essential session cookie (see Cookies below).

Your CV will contain personal information such as your name, contact details and work history. Please leave out anything you wouldn't want processed, and avoid special category data (for example health or ethnicity) unless it is genuinely needed for the role.

Do you have to give us this? No. But we need your CV and the job advert to rewrite your CV, and an email address if you want an account. Without them, we can't do that part for you.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To rewrite your CV, build your skills match report, and let you preview and download the result. This is necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • To transcribe a voice answer, only if you choose to record one. This is based on your consent, and you can always type instead.
  • To sign you in and email you your CV. This is necessary to provide the service.
  • To read your feedback and improve the app. This is our legitimate interest in making Comquat better.

Your Comquat account

A free Comquat account gives you one sign-in for CV Rewrite and Interview Prep. If you create one, we keep:

  • Your name and email address, so you can sign in and we know what to call you.
  • Your password, if you set one, stored only as a scrambled one-way code. We can't see it. You can sign in with an email link instead.
  • Your email choices: which of our newsletters and updates you've ticked, plus a record of when you ticked or unticked each one.
  • Your download confirmations: a record, with the date and time, each time you tick the box to say you've checked your CV or cover letter before downloading it.
  • Your saved documents: a copy of each CV, cover letter and interview prep report you download while signed in, so you can download it again from your account page. Each one holds whatever is in the document, such as your name, contact details and work history.
  • Your master CV, if you build one: everything you type, say or tick in it, such as your name, contact details, your answers to the questions, work history, skills and qualifications, plus the writing style and design you pick and any sample of your own writing you paste in, so you can come back to it and tailor it for each job. It also keeps which way in you chose, the examples you give for your strengths, and your answer to any “just for you” question.
  • Your job search: whether you're looking or have paused your account because you've got a job, when you changed it, and the job titles you save as the jobs you're after.
  • Your photo, if you add one. Before we save it, it's shrunk and any hidden details your phone added, such as where it was taken, are removed. Only you can see it, on your account page. It's never sent to an AI service or shared with anyone.
  • Your monthly check-in choice, if you tick it, with a record of when you ticked or unticked it and when we last sent you one.
  • When you joined, when you last signed in, and when we sent you any “your account will close” emails.

Why, and our legal basis. We use your account details, your master CV, your saved documents, your job search, your photo and the jobs you're after to provide the account you asked for (contract). That includes the emails telling you before we close an unused account. We keep your download confirmations because we have a legitimate interest in being able to show you checked your documents yourself before using them. We only send you the newsletters, updates and monthly check-in you've ticked (consent), and you can untick them at any time from your account page or from the email itself.

The monthly check-in. If you tick it, we email you once a month while you're looking for work, asking how it's going. It has no adverts or newsletters in it. It stops by itself if you pause your account.

Buttons in our emails. Buttons such as Still looking, I've got a job!, Stop these emails and Keep my account work without signing in. Each one does that one thing only: it can't open your account or show your CV, and it stops working after a few weeks.

Share Comquat. If you share Comquat with someone, your message goes through your own phone, email or WhatsApp. We never see who you send it to, and we don't collect your friends' details.

Where it's kept. Your account, your master CV and your saved documents are stored with Supabase, in their London data centre. Only you can see them, and there are no public links to your documents. We only look at your account to help when you ask us to, or to fix a problem.

AI and your details. Nothing in your account (your name, email, password, email choices, master CV or saved documents) is sent to an AI service unless you ask for it, in one of these ways:

  • If you press Write it up for me or Turn this into CV points in the CV builder, the answers or notes for that part are sent to Anthropic to write them up in the style you picked. Your personal details are taken out first.
  • If you record an answer instead of typing, the recording goes to OpenAI as it is, to be turned into text. We don't keep the recording. If you'd rather it didn't go, type instead.
  • If you choose to tailor your master CV for a job, it goes into CV Rewrite like any other CV, and your personal details are taken out before the AI sees it.
  • If you press What did you hear? or Ask me a follow-up, or record an answer in the CV builder, what you said for that question is sent to Anthropic so it can play back what it heard and ask you one more question. Your personal details are taken out first. Pressing Tidy it into one line on a strength works the same way.
  • If you upload a CV to the CV builder, we pick out your name and contact details ourselves, and send the rest to Anthropic to sort into the builder’s sections. The file is deleted as soon as we’ve read it. The health check is worked out by us, not by AI.
  • If you press Suggest jobs for me on your account page, what's in your master CV is sent to Anthropic to suggest jobs it could suit. Your personal details are taken out first, your photo is never sent, and the suggestions are based only on your skills, experience and qualifications.
  • If you press Plan my 90 seconds, what’s in your master CV is sent to Anthropic to turn it into talking notes for your video. Your personal details are taken out first.
  • If you practise on camera in the CV builder, the recording stays on your device. It’s never uploaded and we never see it.
  • Answers to questions marked just for you are never sent to an AI and never go on your CV.

See How we keep your details from the AI below for how the details are taken out.

How long we keep it. Until you delete it. You can delete any saved document or your master CV on its own, or your whole account, from your account page at any time, and it's removed straight away. We also take you off the Comquat emails you ticked. If you signed up to The Loudspeaker or anything else separately on our website, that stays as it is, and you can unsubscribe using the link in any email. If you pause your account because you've got a job, everything stays until you delete it or the 12 month rule below applies. If you don't use your account for 12 months, we'll email you twice, a month before and a week before, each with a button to keep it. If you don't, we close your account and delete everything in it, your photo included. Signing in, using CV Rewrite, Interview Prep or the CV builder, or pressing a button in one of our emails all count as using it.

How we keep your details from the AI

Before your CV, your answers or anything else you give us goes to the AI, we take out your name, email address, phone number, home address and postcode, website and social media links, and date of birth. We swap each one for a placeholder, such as [NAME]. The AI only ever sees the placeholders. When its work comes back, we put your real details back in before you see it.

We can only take out details we can recognise, so please leave out anything else personal that the job doesn't need, such as your referees' contact details. If you record a voice answer, the recording goes to OpenAI as it is to be turned into text, so if you'd rather it didn't, type your answer instead.

No decisions made by computer. We don't make any decisions about you using automated means. The AI drafts your CV, skills match report or interview prep for you to check and change, and you decide what to do with them.

Who processes your data for us

We use a small number of trusted service providers ("processors"), who act only on our instructions:

  • Anthropic: the AI that reads your CV and the advert, rewrites your CV, builds your skills match report and, when you ask, writes up your answers in the CV builder, working on a copy with your personal details taken out (see above).
  • OpenAI: turns a voice answer into text, only if you record one.
  • Supabase: stores your Comquat account, your master CV and your saved documents, and handles signing in, in their London data centre.
  • Resend: sends your sign-in emails, your finished CV if you ask, and any newsletters you've ticked.
  • Render: hosts the app.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where your data is transferred outside the UK, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to train AI models.

CV Rewrite is free and we don't take payments for it. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy before any payment is taken.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded files are deleted from our server as soon as the text has been read out of them.
  • Your CV, advert, answers, photo and skills match report are held only for your working session (up to three hours) so you can tweak and re-download, then discarded. If you're signed in to a Comquat account, a copy of each CV and cover letter you download is kept in your account until you delete it (see Your Comquat account above). Otherwise we don't keep a copy.
  • Your Comquat account is kept until you delete it, or until it has gone unused for 12 months (see Your Comquat account above).
  • Feedback is kept so we can learn from it. Ask us and we'll delete yours.
  • Emails we send you may stay in our email system as a record.

Young people

Comquat is for anyone aged 16 or over, and many people use it to build their first CV while they're still at school or college. We follow the Information Commissioner's Office's Children's code: accounts are private by default, emails are off unless you tick them, there's no messaging, no adverts and no tracking, and we collect as little as we can. You confirm you're 16 or older when you create an account, and we don't ask anyone's exact age. See Keeping you safe for how we protect young people and how to report a concern.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. If you have a Comquat account, you can download everything it holds, correct your name and delete the account yourself from your account page. For anything else, email us at the address above. Unless you've saved documents to a Comquat account, we delete your CV data quickly, so there may be little left to act on once your session has ended.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, tell us first by emailing the address above with "Data protection complaint" in the subject line. We'll confirm we've got it within 30 days, and keep you posted while we look into it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

We use one essential "session" cookie so the app can remember your progress while you use it. If you sign in to a Comquat account, we also use essential sign-in cookies that keep you signed in across CV Rewrite and Interview Prep. We don't use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, so there's nothing to opt into.

Security

The app runs over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and we limit how long your data exists. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
