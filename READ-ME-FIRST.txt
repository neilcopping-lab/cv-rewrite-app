CV REWRITE - PERSONALITY, SMARTER QUESTIONS + EMAIL-YOUR-CV UPDATE

WHAT'S NEW
1) Fun loading messages: while the AI works, 8 warm messages pop up in a random
   order ("Pop the kettle on, this bit can take a minute or two." etc.).
2) No more asking for things already on your CV: the question step now only asks
   about genuine gaps. If your CV already covers everything, it says "Nothing to
   add - press Write my CV" instead of inventing questions. All questions are
   clearly optional.
3) Your CV is now EMAILED to you: on your first download of a CV, the Word and
   PDF are attached to your confirmation email (as well as downloading in the
   browser). NOTE: emails only send if RESEND_API_KEY is set AND your sending
   domain is verified in Resend - otherwise nothing can be emailed (including the
   sign-in link).

Also includes the earlier fixes: the "leave them all out" stuck-button fix, the
font sizes matched to the main site, and the Creative design / photo / links /
regen-cap features.
