CV REWRITE - ACCOUNTS & CREDIT PACKS UPDATE
============================================

WHAT'S NEW
- Pricing is now PACKS: £5 = 4 CVs, £10 = 10 CVs.
- People sign in with their EMAIL (a link is emailed to them - no passwords).
- Their credit balance is saved to their account and does not expire.
- Downloading a finished CV uses ONE credit. All formats of that same CV
  (Word, PDF, ATS) share the one credit. Regenerating a new CV uses another.
- Privacy Policy and Terms updated to describe the packs/credits.

PART A - PUT THE NEW CODE IN (same as before)
1. Unzip this folder.
2. In your cv-rewrite-app folder, replace by dragging these in (choose Replace):
      - the "lib" folder
      - the "public" folder
      - "server.js"
      - "render.yaml"
      - "DEPLOY.md"
3. GitHub Desktop: Commit to main, Push origin. Wait for Render to go Live.

PART B - TWO NEW THINGS IN RENDER (needed for accounts to work properly)

1) ADD A DISK (so balances survive restarts):
   Render -> your service -> Settings -> Disks -> Add Disk
      Name: data
      Mount Path: /data
      Size: 1 GB
   Save. (Render will restart the service.)

2) ADD ONE ENVIRONMENT VARIABLE:
   Render -> your service -> Environment -> Add:
      Key:   DB_PATH
      Value: /data/app.json
   Save.

3) MAKE SURE RESEND IS SET:
   Sign-in links are emailed, so RESEND_API_KEY must be filled in
   (Render -> Environment). Without it, people can't sign in.

The Stripe webhook you already set up now grants the credits automatically.
Nothing else to change in Stripe (still the checkout.session.completed event).
