Keep a copy of your profile

Save the profile your recruiter made with you, so it's always to hand.

What happens when you save it

  • A copy of your profile goes into My documents in your Comquat account. You can read it, download it or delete it whenever you like.
  • It's private. Only you can see it. Nothing in your account is shared with employers or recruiters.
  • Your recruiter keeps their own copy, as they told you. Saving one here doesn't change anything they do with theirs.

What else your free account gives you

  • CV Rewrite: your CV rewritten for the job you're going for, without inventing a thing.
  • Interview Prep: a report on the company, your opening pitch and the questions to get ready for.
  • CV Builder: build one full CV, then tailor it for every job.
  • Pitch Video: plan, practise and record a short video about you.

You can close your account at any time, and everything in it goes with it. How we look after your information.
